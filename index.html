Phaser classroom demo

PidgeonWorld

Guide Pixel the pigeon through a floating garden, collect the seeds that match the current lesson target, and dodge swooping hawks. The HUD keeps your learners informed about goals, streaks, and math tips.

  • Arrow Keys Move Pixel
  • Space Dash burst
  • M Toggle music cues

Quick tips

  1. Collect only the highlighted numbers.
  2. Build streaks to earn bonus points.
  3. Use dashes to escape hawks.

Every round loops through differentiated lesson cards so you can scale difficulty up or down in seconds.
